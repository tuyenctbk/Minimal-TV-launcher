Family & Privacy First

Privacy Policy

Application: Minimal TV Launcher
Effective Date: September 5, 2026

1. Introduction & Core Commitment

Minimal TV Launcher ("we", "our", or "the app") is designed specifically for Android TV and Google TV living room environments with family safety, distraction-free entertainment, and child wellness in mind. We believe privacy is a fundamental right.

Our principle is simple: We do not collect, store, transmit, monetize, or sell any personal data, usage habits, device identifiers, or biometric information.

2. Information Collection and Storage

Minimal TV Launcher functions as an offline-first launcher application. All operational configurations remain exclusively on your local device:

  • Application Catalog: The app queries the local Android package manager solely to display installed TV applications on your home screen. This list is processed locally and is never sent to external servers.
  • Parental Controls & Settings: Screen time limits, bedtime schedules, customized app categories, PIN protections, and local activity counters are saved locally in private device storage (Android DataStore / SharedPreferences).
  • No Telemetry or Tracking: The app contains no third-party tracking SDKs, no behavioral telemetry, no crash reporting beacons with personal data, and no targeted advertising frameworks.

3. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

We strictly adhere to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GDPR Kids protection guidelines, and Google Play Families Policy:

  • We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children of any age.
  • The launcher provides robust, PIN-protected screen time timers and bedtime restrictions to help parents supervise TV usage.
  • No online accounts or social media logins are required to use the launcher.

4. Permissions Used

Minimal TV Launcher requests only the standard system permissions necessary to function as a TV home launcher:

  • QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES: Required to index and display installed Android TV applications in categories (Streaming, Games, Education, Tools).
  • SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW: Used solely to display the full-screen Bedtime Lock and Screen Time Limit notice when daily limits expire.

5. Third-Party Links & External Applications

Launching a third-party application (such as Netflix, YouTube, or educational games) transfers control to that respective application. Those third-party apps are governed by their own individual privacy policies and terms of service.

6. Security of Your Data

Parental PIN codes and supervisor configurations are encrypted and protected using standard Android security mechanisms within the application's isolated sandbox storage.

7. Changes to This Policy

If updates are made to this Privacy Policy, the revised document will be updated within the application and accompanying app repository with a revised effective date.
